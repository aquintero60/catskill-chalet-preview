A letter from the chalet

On long-weekend arrivals and the quiet luxury of having room for everyone

There’s a moment, just after the cars are unpacked and the last tote is carried inside, when the house exhales. Boots line up by the door, someone finds the kettle, and the Catskills feel close enough to touch through the windows. We created The Catskill Chalet for that moment—an easy, elevated retreat at the end of a private drive, a short walk to downtown Tannersville and minutes from Hunter Mountain, where a group can settle in without feeling stacked on top of one another.

Read the rest of the letter

The layout is intentional: spacious bedrooms plus a den, five bathrooms, three living rooms, and two full kitchens—so two families can keep their rhythms, or a bigger crew can spread out and still come together. Coffee can happen in one kitchen while pancakes happen in the other; kids can play downstairs while the grown-ups linger by the fire upstairs; someone can sneak away for a chapter and a view.

Days here are meant to be chosen, not scheduled. Ski laps and après in winter. Hikes, swimming holes, and slow dinners in summer. A quick walk for pastries, a last-minute reservation, a farmers market stop—then back to the calm of the house. And when the light drops, the hot tub becomes its own ritual: a private, steamy pause beneath the trees before everyone drifts back inside.

What we hope you feel most is ease—design that’s modern and warm, details that hold up to real use, and spaces that invite celebration without demanding it. Whether you’re marking a milestone, gathering multiple families, or simply escaping the city for air and quiet, the Chalet is built to make togetherness feel effortless.

If you come, come fully—bring the extra board game, the good bottle, the people you miss. We’ll have the lights on.
